How this comparison works

Recall compares your inputs with a small, manually reviewed catalog of first-party pricing and policy sources. Matching and cost calculations are rules-based, not an AI or GenLayer verdict. This is not an exhaustive market search or an accuracy ranking.

Estimates exclude taxes, free credits, unselected add-ons, integration costs and negotiated volume discounts. “From” rates and unconfirmed privacy configurations are never treated as firm quotes. Review dates are fixed; fetching a page does not revalidate its meaning.

No provider is contacted, no order is placed, and no customer audio is uploaded. Public provider plans are not signed offers in Recall. Your saved options stay in this browser, separately from your Studio purchases.
