# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People delegating subscriptions and operational payments to AI agents. They need a fast way to define what an agent may pay, verify a proposal, and prevent unauthorized funds from moving.

## Product Purpose

IntentLatch is a decentralized payment firewall for AI agents. A user writes a natural-language mandate, an agent proposes a structured payment, and a GenLayer Intelligent Contract returns an APPROVE, REJECT, or MANUAL_REVIEW verdict. An approval creates a requester-bound, exact-value, single-use permit; only consuming that permit can release test GEN to the locked recipient. Success means a first-time user can understand the value, create a mandate, authorize a payment, and see the execution boundary in under one minute.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy. The product communicates safety and authority without looking bureaucratic, alarmist, or speculative.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic crypto dashboards, neon cyberpunk styling, glowing gradients, glass panels, excessive wallet jargon, marketing-heavy landing pages, and interfaces that hide the working demonstration below a hero section.

## Design Principles

- Put the authorization task in the first viewport.
- Make every verdict understandable through words, symbols, and color.
- Show the exact rule and evidence behind a decision.
- Keep blockchain complexity behind familiar product language.
- Demonstrate real consequences with small test-GEN transfers and explicit finalization states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, high contrast, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and verdict indicators that do not rely on color alone.
