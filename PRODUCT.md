# Recall

Register: product.

September 8 interface refinement: compact account settings, readable proposal names and dated receipt events replace expanded form-like controls. The saved unsigned reply is retained under Details as historical material; it is not the signed purchase state. This changes no contracts, request storage, wallet journal, receipt matching or signing authority. Direct first-party component research informed this revision.

New product direction: a buyer specifies what they want to purchase, their budget, and custom conditions; a supplier replies with a price and public terms; the buyer reviews evidence before a separately wallet-approved purchase. EU-only processing is an optional template, not the product's fixed purpose.

The new `/workspace` implements browser-local drafts, unsigned supplier links with matching-request checks, and a two-party wallet-controlled Studio flow. After creating an agreement, the supplier signs acceptance; the buyer requests assessment, approves without sending funds, and may pay separately after review. Either participant can report changed terms before the review closes. The buyer may cancel and the supplier may offer a separately assessed replacement. No automated agent, server-side inbox, custody, or real-world delivery attestation is provided.

The two-party contract and wallet adapter have direct-VM, offline RPC, and isolated fake-wallet browser tests. The September 7 user run reached deployment and supplier acceptance; the public acceptance receipt was independently confirmed successful. The complete new purchase/payment run remains unvalidated. The older recorded proof and four-role wallet test below remain separate. A persisted transaction journal and cross-tab lock prevent automatic resubmission. Known hashes are checked automatically while the page is visible; a missing hash requires explicit wallet-activity recovery. Pending results block signing, never wallet connection. Payment claims require a matching finalized recipient transfer, not merely a consumed approval.

The September 7 usability revision preserves existing drafts, links, journal and contract source. It detects already-authorized accounts without permission prompts, invalidates a review on account/network changes, and shows the buyer/supplier role. Read-only updates pause during editing/review and in hidden tabs, with backoff on failure. No signing is automatic. The next action comes first; request details, offer history and technical records use progressive disclosure. Users can test both roles on one purchase page by selecting accounts in their wallet extension.

The purchase detail now puts price and the next action beside its evidence on desktop, above it on mobile. Terms review, offers, activity and details are separate keyboard-accessible tabs. Pending or missing results remain visible outside the tabs. Inter is served locally with its license to keep the existing typography consistent across browsers. This changes presentation, not contract rules or persisted purchase data.

People inspecting agent purchase authorizations need to understand which evidence supports a permit, what changed, and whether a payment was actually verified. Recall is an evidence-dependent payment-permit feasibility prototype, not a finished commercial purchasing service.

The inspection interface separates the exported hosted-Studio run from local direct-VM replay with scripted web/AI fixtures. Check Studio compares fresh public reads without replacing the historical view. Matched, mismatched and unavailable results are distinct and timestamped; separate latest reads are not an atomic snapshot or continuous monitoring. Recorded data is never presented as a fresh network check. Scheduled messages alone never establish recipient payment.

The separate /purchase flow prepares bounded unsigned requests for the user's browser wallet: deployment, seller acceptance/publication, evaluation, reservation, challenge, resolution, cancellation and exact payment. Four distinct onchain roles remain explicit. Every write requires a reviewed request and wallet approval. The server has no signing authority or keys. Pending and uncertain outcomes block new requests; browser-local public history supports recovery without automatic resubmission. The September 6–7 user-approved browser-wallet run completed original cancellation and a separately reviewed 0.040 test-GEN replacement payment. Eleven known receipts and the recipient transfer were independently checked; the original reservation hash was not captured. This is one hosted-Studio sandbox run, not general reliability or production validation.

Personality: precise, calm, compact, trustworthy. Put actual purchases, the evidence comparison, and the financial consequence ahead of marketing. Explain claim, permit, and payment as different objects. Keep the canceled original and separately reviewed replacement distinct.

Target desktop inspection first, with full keyboard access and responsive phone/tablet layouts. Preserve accessible contrast and state labels; use smaller typography without removing readable text or touch targets.
