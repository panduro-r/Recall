# Recall — uncoached decision test

Status: prepared September 14, 2026; **not yet run with a participant**. This is not a claim that the product passed usability testing.

## Setup

Ask one person unfamiliar with Recall to try the product for 5–10 minutes. Use the owner's prepared browser containing the two real saved reviews, or a supervised screen-sharing session with permission. Do not share wallet credentials, fabricate records or connect a participant's wallet. No new assessment, provider signup, email, purchase or payment is needed. Record notes; screen recording requires the participant's consent.

Say only:

> Your team needs an API to transcribe 100 hours of English prerecorded audio per month. The budget is $50, and customer audio must not be used for model training. Compare Speechmatics and AssemblyAI, explain what still needs confirming, and produce something you could send a teammate to support your decision.

Do not name buttons or guide them through the app. If they get stuck, note the point of confusion before helping. Stop any action that would submit a transaction or disclose private data.

## Observe

- Can they find the side-by-side comparison and change either provider?
- Are the workload and requirements retained when opening a review and returning?
- Do they distinguish an illustrative base cost from a confirmed eligible price?
- Can they find the AssemblyAI required setup and its evidence without coaching?
- Do they understand that a supported document finding does not verify actual account settings or service quality?
- Can they export the HTML comparison and explain what it contains?
- Does a reload preserve the same saved review? No resubmission should be required.

Provisional success gate, not a research finding: finish within ten minutes without coaching, identify the price/setup uncertainty, export a readable report, and avoid any accidental wallet action. Do not require them to choose a particular provider; a reasoned “need more information” is a valid decision.
