Create one distinctive compact geometric capital R monogram. The letter should feel purpose-drawn from a confident continuous folded band: a rounded upper bowl, strong upright, decisive diagonal leg, and a single clean negative-space cut suggesting a checkpoint. It must read immediately as R, not B, P, an arrow, a refresh symbol or a currency symbol. Restrained, exceptionally crisp flat vector-like graphic rendered as PNG, optically balanced and recognizable at 16px. White monogram on a solid Cobalt blue #2554D9 square tile with subtly rounded corners (corner radius about 20 percent); outside those rounded corners must be genuinely transparent. Square 1024x1024 canvas, tile reaches the canvas edges, white mark centered with approximately 20 percent internal padding on every side. One asset only, no wordmark, no caption, no variants, no mockup, no shadows, no gradients, no texture, no bevel, no 3D, no decorative dots. This replaces a dated plum R-with-return-arrow logo; do not reproduce a return arrow or plum color.
