## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

**Show:** Recall comparison and requirements.

“Choosing an AI API is more than comparing its headline price. Can it handle my workload? Will it train on my customer data? Does meeting my conditions require a different plan or an opt-out? Recall helps teams compare services with evidence before they commit.”

## 0:20–0:45 — A practical comparison

**Show:** Compare options, then select two alternatives. Keep price caveats visible.

“Here I need a transcription API for one hundred hours a month, within fifty dollars, without model training. **Show:** Studio Next explorer, the contract address and finalized deployment. This is an existing real deployment, not an on-screen fresh transaction.
